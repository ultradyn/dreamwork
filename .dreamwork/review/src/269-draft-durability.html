Two calls — cross-tab conflict, and how long to keep orphans

The rest of the design is settled without you (logical id, clear-on-receipt seam, localStorage-first, no #263 lanes). Two policies touch your words and your multi-window habits, so they are yours:

  1. C1 — cross-tab when two unfocused boxes disagree. Rec: R1 — never overwrite a focused field; when unfocused texts diverge, offer "updated in another tab — load that version?" rather than silent last-write-wins. Cross-tab — focus-wins, not last-write-wins on your caret

A focused (or dirty-since-focus) field is never overwritten by another tab's autosave. The store still updates so reload is safe. Peers learn via storage events (BroadcastChannel only if/when IndexedDB lands). Suspended tabs keep last synced save; offline is irrelevant because drafts never leave the browser.
