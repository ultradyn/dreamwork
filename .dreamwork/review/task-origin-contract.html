Decision review · task #213

Make task origin explicit
without inventing history.

The ledger can only classify a small fraction of past tasks. The proposed contract starts recording origin at the next permanent id, preserves unknown history as unknown, and makes omission fail loudly from that point onward.

forward-onlyhuman / looplint-enforcedno backfill

The decision

Recommendation: add an inline origin: **human|loop** field to every newly minted file-ledger task, plus an Origin required from cutoff in the ledger header. For this repository the cutoff is permanent id #216.
Next id: **216**
Origin required from: **216**

- **#216** — Example human steer · P2 · task · 20m · origin: **human**
- **#217** — Example loop discovery · P3 · idea · 15m · origin: **loop**

Meaning: origin records who initiated the task. It is not the owner, approver, executor, or current decision-maker. A human approving a loop idea does not turn it into a human-originated task.

Why this shape survives

Context: a prose ledger is already durable, permanent ids already order the history, and older tasks cannot be classified reliably. The contract must improve future truth without laundering guesses into facts.

Exact semantics

  • Allowed values are exactly human and loop.
  • Every id at or above the cutoff has exactly one origin.
  • Tasks below the cutoff remain legal without origin; no historical entry is rewritten.
  • A legacy ledger without a cutoff warns rather than errors, preserving compatibility while announcing incomplete enforcement.
  • The cutoff cannot exceed Next id, which would create an unenforced gap.
