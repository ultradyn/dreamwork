The problem (the reconciliation, which is the hard half)

run-mode is one line from a closed set — lackadaisical / hot / assisted — and that single axis carries at least two independent decisions: how often the loop acts (pace) and whether it acts through subagents (delegation). assisted is the only value that implies helpers, and it also implies a pace, so "lackadaisical but delegating" is unexpressible. Tonight's session was exactly that state, held in conversation rather than the file — so it did not survive a restart. Your #445 adds a third axis: how much you are asked (the four levels). Whether these are one control or several is decided by the IGC below.

The four levels — what surfaces / is emitted / is logged / no-reply

Two rules run through all four, stated once: (i) the escalation test is materiality against your goals, not difficulty — "some choices where you have multiple good options … are not very material … unless the user has specifically mentioned something"; and (ii) the IGC evaluation happens at every level — shown to you at 1, embedded in the emitted doc at 2, logged unsurfaced at 3, applied silently at 4.

2 · keep me informed

surfaces mostly automatic; each material choice emits documentation, not a question. "a review for the human's review, but it's not asking them for a choice." ~10–20% of questions escalate — a soft estimate that steers, never a counter that gates (your 01:17 ruling).

no reply nothing parks on you — the doc is not an ask, so work proceeds; only the escalated minority blocks.

3 · near-automatic

surfaces nothing, unless genuinely big or stuck. "too much in the noise to actually surface."
emitted nothing routinely; the IGC still happens and is logged to a journal folder (ADR-shaped; proposed home .dreamwork/docs/journal/ — Q2/Q3).
no reply nothing parks; proceed with everything. Stuck is a state you earn — before declaring a blocker, use a subagent to research the question: has anyone solved it, what are the options. Only a still-stuck-after-research item surfaces, then like L1/L2. The goals move: if stuck, ask about your goals rather than the immediate decision.

4 · full auto

surfaces nothing routinely. "tasked with figure it out." A question reaches you only for a cheap unblocker you alone can resolve ("do we have a domain?"), raised as early as possible.
no reply never blocked on a reply — every blocker is the loop's to solve; keep working the unanswered question in the background, and don't rabbit-hole while other work exists.
cooperation "you still want to cooperate with the user … but you never want to be blocked just because the user hasn't replied." Never contradict your goals — don't buy a domain for a project that already has one.

Proposed DREAMWORK.md wording (the ask-about-goals move)

The dictation places this at L3; its reasoning generalises, so it belongs in DREAMWORK.md as a preference that applies at every level. The coordinator owns DREAMWORK.md — this is the proposed wording, not an edit:

When uncertain, ask about goals, not just the immediate decision. Uncertainty usually means the goals need to be more specific. Knowing his goals lets the loop evaluate not just the current choice but many future ones, so when stuck or torn, prefer a question that sharpens the goals over one that resolves only the immediate call.
