# Dream — first live dogfood, reflection (session of 2026-07-24/25)

The skill was built and then immediately lived-in for ~2.5 hours with the
human present and steering. Distilled:

## What held up

- **Small increments + immediate re-read caught real errors**: the
  reconcile-before-baseline ordering flaw, a stale cross-reference, a
  garbled sentence caught pre-commit. The error-catching philosophy is
  not aspirational — it fired repeatedly.
- **The questions spine earned its place the moment it existed**: the
  D-veto ask survived the human going afk; "chat is not durable" proved
  itself the same night it was written.
- **Dreamer mechanics worked first try**: constrained brainstorm, useful
  dream file (it found the missing `.dreamwork/` scaffold, the surprise
  git repo, the aspirational test-suite language). Its contradicting
  observation (git repo exists) was *correct* and my cached fact was
  stale — verify before dismissing a subagent's contradiction.
- **Steering verbs got real exercise**: `do next` queue-jumps, many
  `add idea:`s, one veto, "rec" approvals. Capture-is-cheap meant zero
  lost ideas and one clean deletion.
- **Mismatched-signals guardrail made the save of the night**: wrong-cwd
  trap (another project's `.backlog/` nearly became our backend).

## Friction observed

- **Presence-awareness is unstated**: when the human is actively
  streaming ideas, starting autonomous increments risks collision; the
  right behavior (observed, improvised) is capture-only until the stream
  pauses. Filed as a task to encode.
- **Facts that gate behavior go stale**: "not a git repo" flipped mid-
  session. Reconcile should re-check such facts from the world, not from
  conversational memory.
- **Teammate idle notifications are ambiguous** (needed one nudge to
  start; second idle was terminal). Dispatch prompts should — and now do
  — demand the final message be the raw report with durable-state
  changes listed.
- **Untested edges**: the 15–20 min cap never forced a split tonight;
  compaction survival and cross-session resume are still unexercised.
